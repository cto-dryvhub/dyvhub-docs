# Send feedback

This page explains how anyone on a dealership can tell DryvHub about a bug or an idea from the dashboard.

:::tip[In plain English]
In the sidebar, under **Verification**, choose **Send feedback**, pick **Feedback** or **Bug**, describe what happened, and optionally paste a Google Drive (or other) link. DryvHub keeps a copy for your dealership and emails support.
:::

## Who can send it

**Owner**, **admin**, and **staff** can all open **Send feedback**. It sits in the secondary side nav below Verification (staff who do not see Verification still see Send feedback below Settings).

## How to send a report

1. In the sidebar, choose **Send feedback** (below Verification).
2. Pick **Feedback** or **Bug**.
3. Enter a **Subject** (at least 3 characters) and **Details** (at least 10 characters).
4. Optionally paste an **Evidence link** — an `https` URL only, such as a Google Drive file. There is no file upload.
5. Confirm **Includes this page** shows the page you are on. DryvHub stores the path without any query string.
6. Press **Send**.

On success you see a short thank-you and a **reference** (the first 8 characters of the report id). Keep that reference if you follow up with support.

### Phone layout

Open the sidebar if it is closed, then choose **Send feedback**. The dialog uses the full width of the content area. The page path stays on one line. Cancel and Send stay on one row.

## Limits

- You can send up to **5** reports per hour. After that the dialog shows: "You have sent several reports recently. Try again in an hour."
- Subject max 120 characters. Details max 4,000 characters. Evidence link max 500 characters and must be `http` or `https`.

## What happens next

DryvHub stores the report against your dealership and tries to email the support inbox. If email delivery fails, the report is still saved so platform staff can read it in the admin portal. Dealers do not get a history list of past reports in the dashboard.
